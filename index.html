HOW IT WORKS

League Information

Season 1 · Nov 14, 2026 — Dec 2027

MBG's Gym Leader Challenge League is a casual, year-long season. We'll aim to play every other Saturday after regular Pokémon League. Events are for fun and are not sanctioned Play! Pokémon events.

THE CHALLENGE

Collect all 10 badges.

Win with each of the 10 GLC types to earn that type's Gym Badge. The first player to collect all 10 wins the Season 1 grand prize.

Fairy wins are tracked as a bonus badge, but are not required for season completion.

SEASON ENTRY

$20 for Season 1

The entry covers the full season. Funds support Gym Badges, rental decks, and the prize for the first player to complete all 10 badges.

LEAGUE RULES

Quick Rules

Proxies: Allowed.

Non-English cards: Allowed with a translation, provided card backs cannot be distinguished through sleeves.

Town Map: If both players agree, Prize Cards may be viewed before the game, then shuffled before being placed back down.
